# `data/` — what is in here, who owns it, and under what terms

This directory mixes three different things, and they are **not** all under the same licence:

1. **Files this project generated.** Selections, splits, labels, fitted thresholds and predictions.
   Licensed **CC BY 4.0**, © 2026 Isac Smith — see [`data/LICENSE`](LICENSE).
2. **Files that carry values derived from someone else's dataset.** Materials Project ids, space
   groups, hull distances and DFT energies; WBM material ids. These are *adaptations* of CC BY 4.0
   datasets. They are redistributable, and they remain subject to the original attribution
   requirement, which travels with the file.
3. **Files that are not this project's to license at all.** Two tables transcribed from published
   papers, and one table of published benchmark metrics. These are **carved out** of the CC BY 4.0
   grant and are listed separately below.

Nothing here is a bulk redistribution of a third-party dataset. The Materials Project responses and
the WBM data files live in `cache/`, which is gitignored and never published; the setup instructions
tell you to fetch them yourself.

**The caveat on the two experimental CSVs.** Individual measured constants are facts and are not
themselves copyrightable in the US; what copyright can reach is the *selection and arrangement* of a
published table, and these reproduce a substantial part of the arrangement of two specific tables.
The assessment in [`reports/licensing.md`](../reports/licensing.md) §1.4 is **low risk and standard
scientific practice** — these are benchmark reference sets that exist to be reused, and both files
carry the full citation in their header — **but it is the closest judgement call in this
repository.** They are kept, cited, and explicitly not relicensed. If you want that risk at zero,
replace them with a script that reconstructs the tables from the papers the reader fetches
themselves; that costs reproducibility for anyone without journal access, which is why it is not the
default.

## A note on the `harness_commit` fields

Several files here record the commit that produced them, as `"harness_commit": "<short sha>"`.

Before this repository was first published, its history was rewritten once to replace the author's
personal email address with a GitHub noreply address. That rewrite changed every commit hash while
leaving every tree byte-identical — same files, same content, new commit names. The
`harness_commit` values in this directory were rebased to the new hashes at the same time, so that
each one still resolves to the commit whose tree actually produced the file. No data value was
changed by that edit.
