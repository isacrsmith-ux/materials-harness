## What was compared, and what Phase 0 allowed

The one static quantity the feasibility memo (`reports/aerospace_durability_feasibility.md`) allows for
this comparison is the **bulk oxide formation energy per O atom** on the harness's MP-corrected
convention: the thermodynamic driving force for oxidation. For each metal the flight rows cover, every
binary oxide on the MP GGA/GGA+U hull was relaxed with each engine and placed exactly as mode (a) places
a candidate (MP2020 corrections, MP's O reference). The O reference is shared by all metals, so its error
cannot reorder them.

**Said plainly, where the comparison is limited by Phase 0:**

- **Formation energy is not a rate.** The flight quantities are outcomes of oxide growth, spallation,
  temperature and flux over the exposure. Nothing computed here models any of that, so no agreement or
  disagreement below can be read as prediction.
- **Impact and cascade physics is left out entirely.** Both engines fail at close approach (memo §3), so
  no quantity involving an energetic O impact or a displacement cascade was computed.
- **Defect formation energies are left out.** Phase 0 found them usable, but neither flight source
  measures radiation damage, so there is no flight counterpart to set them against.
- **Surface chemisorption is left out.** The engine represents it statically (memo §2), but it was
  validated on one surface, Ag(111), and the flight samples are polycrystalline.
- **The Al-Li alloys are represented by Al and Li separately.** The source gives no composition, and
  attributes their mass loss to lithium loss, not oxidation.

## Open decisions

1. **Is the engine usable, or is a different tool needed?** Static energies, yes, at a descriptive tier
   and production engine only (memo). Everything the flight data measures is kinetic or impact-driven,
   so the real question needs MD with a short-range-corrected potential. **Not built; this is the
   recommendation coming back to you.**
2. **MISSE is not accessible,** and its metal fraction is unknown. `materialsinspace.nasa.gov` does not
   resolve. MAPTIS requires registration, paid for non-NASA users since January 2026. The public Glenn
   subset is polymers and coatings. Ingestion work is not justified unless access is arranged.
3. **Which alloy first?** The single-crystal Ag/Cu case has no numbers in any retrieved document. The
   usable choice is between EOIM-3's Al-Li rows and the pure metals:
   - **Al-Li rows:** structurally relevant, but composition-less, with alloy and temperature confounded
     and a Δm/A that cannot be reconstructed.
   - **Pure metals:** cleaner, but 7 of 15 pure-metal Δm values are within 2× the
     printed ±0.02 mg of zero.
4. **The paused stability-screening track** (f-electron confirmation, intermetallic, OQMD held-out):
   resume in parallel, or stay parked? Nothing here competes for its compute.
5. **Flight values in public reports.** The sources are US-Government public-use documents, and
   `reference_data/space_ao_erosion.csv` already commits MISSE-2 values, but this phase's rule kept them
   gitignored. Allow committing the side-by-side table?
